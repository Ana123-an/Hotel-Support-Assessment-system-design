# Support Automation Assessment: Founder's Office Intern

A design for taking a hospitality SaaS company's Intercom support from ~70% automated resolution to ~85%+, with every remaining human handoff chosen by a rule and delivered with a full briefing.

## What's inside

- `support-automation-assessment.md`: the full document:
  - reading the problem and the questions I'd ask
  - system architecture (Mermaid diagram included)
  - decision rule and the "never automate" list
  - implementation, rollout plan and withdrawal thresholds
  - build/buy/delegate, cost and the resolution-rate commitment

## Notes

All figures (costs, thresholds, percentages) are flagged estimates, not measurements. Assumptions are marked **[A]** and unknowns **[?]** in the document.
